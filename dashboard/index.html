About the data

  • Sessions run August to July. Tables that use a calendar year ("2019") match session "2019-20" and are merged with it.
  • Since about 2018, ministry answers use "trained" to mean "enrolled". Those figures appear here as enrolment. Completions (trained) and certifications exist only up to 2017-18.
  • Where answers disagree, the most recently published figure is used. Values marked revised had other figures in earlier answers.
  • The DGT dashboard covers admission years 2019 to 2026 but holds only part of the candidates before 2023 (10% to 75% of data.gov.in enrolment).
  • Not available here: district, institute or trade-level data for all states, and assessed or passed counts by state. Those are on the DGT dashboard's Matrix Data page.
