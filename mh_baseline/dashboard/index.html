About this baseline

  • Source: a government-supplied sheet of all Maharashtra government ITIs ( institutes across districts), covering seat capacity and enrolment by gender for 2023, 2024 and 2025.
  • Enrolled always equals seats, for every institute and year — the enrolled-by-gender columns read as a gender split of seat capacity, not an independently measured enrolment figure. Treat this as reported intake, not a fill rate, until the department confirms what the field means.
  • Pass rates, trainer vacancy and qualifications, dropout, employability-skills scores, and industry linkage (MSMEs engaged, active MoUs) are tracked columns with no data yet for any institute; see “Indicator coverage” above.
  • One institute (Government Higher Level ITI, Chembur, Mumbai Suburban) opened in 2025 and has no 2023/2024 figures — it is included with those years blank.
  • “Gender ratio” / “female share” on this page means female enrolment as a percentage of total enrolment, not a sex-ratio (females per 1,000 males).
  • District boundaries on the map are from Survey of India (via opencity.in, Public Domain), simplified for the web; names predate the 2021–2024 district renames and are normalised to match this baseline (see mh_baseline/build_geo.py).
